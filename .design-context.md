## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are pharmacy staff (pharmacists and assistants) who perform regular drug inventory counts. They work in a pharmacy environment, needing a reliable, fast, and accurate way to verify stock levels against expected quantities. Their goal is to minimize manual errors, reduce counting time, and maintain audit-ready records. The interface should instill confidence, reduce cognitive load, and support quick decision-making under potentially busy conditions.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, modern, precise, and slightly futuristic. The brand conveys technological competence while remaining approachable for healthcare professionals. It should feel secure and dependable, with a touch of innovation that differentiates from traditional paper-based processes.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode sci‑tech theme with neon accent colors. Background: deep blue (#07142b). Card surfaces: blurred dark slate (#162a56) with glassmorphism. Accent / success: vivid cyan (#00f2fe) for highlights, interactive states, and progress indicators. Alert / missing: neon red (#ff4b5c) for errors and mismatches. Typography: clean sans‑serif, UI‑focused, with clear hierarchy. Motion: purposeful, subtle animations (breathe, shimmer, scan lines) that convey activity without distraction. Overall feel: high‑tech, dashboard‑like, optimized for mobile use.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration** – Every visual element must serve a functional purpose; avoid purely decorative motifs.
2. **Mobile‑first interaction** – Primary use is via smartphone; touch targets are generous, and core actions (scan, confirm, capture) are reachable with one thumb.
3. **Immediate feedback** – User actions (barcode match, photo upload, status change) trigger instant visual responses (color shifts, animations, haptic‑like cues).
4. **Error‑tolerant & guided** – Mistakes are highlighted clearly, with inline guidance for correction (e.g., quantity input after mismatch).
5. **Consistent visual language** – Reuse of component styles (tech‑card, tech‑input, tech‑button) and animations across pages ensures predictability.
6. **Accessibility first** – Sufficient contrast (WCAG AA), support for reduced motion preferences, and readable text scaling.
7. **Data‑driven validation** – Real‑time validation against expected quantities prevents accumulation of errors.
8. **Audit‑ready evidence** – Photo capture is integrated seamlessly, with clear indicators of pending or completed capture.
